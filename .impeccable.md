## Design Context

### Users
People querying and maintaining a local evidence workspace. Their tasks are source ingestion, retrieval, exact evidence inspection, job recovery, and retrieval evaluation. Source: HANDOFF and the dashboard design supplied by the owner.

### Brand Personality
Careful, calm, precise. Convey confidence through visible provenance and honest coverage, without implying retrieved evidence is an automatically correct answer.

### Aesthetic Direction
The owner explicitly selected a quiet, dense workbench with warm neutrals and a restrained accent. Continue Tychora's forest-green identity. Use compact navigation and tables, an open reading surface, local typography, minimal motion, and no generic metric-card grid.

### Design Principles
- Evidence and exact provenance come before technical scores.
- Use density for navigation and inventory, breathing room for reading.
- Incomplete, unavailable, and unknown states have distinct words.
- Keyboard and narrow-screen users retain every inspection function.
- Imported content and credentials never become browser-persisted state.
